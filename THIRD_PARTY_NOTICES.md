# Third-party OCR assets

FastDict packages [Tesseract.js](https://github.com/naptha/tesseract.js), [Tesseract.js Core](https://github.com/naptha/tesseract.js-core), and English, simplified/traditional Chinese, Japanese and Korean models from [tessdata_fast](https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tessdata_fast). These projects and models are distributed under Apache License 2.0. The asset preparation script includes license copies in the built application under `ocr/`.

Images are processed on-device. OCR assets are downloaded during development/build preparation and are loaded locally by the installed application.
